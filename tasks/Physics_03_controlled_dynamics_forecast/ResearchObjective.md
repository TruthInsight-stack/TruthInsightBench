# Frequency- and Time-Domain Boundaries of Controlled-Dynamics Forecasts

- Task ID: `Physics_03_controlled_dynamics_forecast`
- T0: `2021-04-01`

## Research objective

Using paired reference and forecast responses under multiple control conditions, quantify agreement in frequency features and in complete time-domain trajectories separately. Determine whether the two evidence channels agree, conflict, or change by condition, and do not substitute random time-point splits for extrapolation to complete held-out conditions.
