# Accuracy and Applicability Boundaries of Classical Representations for Variational Quantum Circuits

- Task ID: `Physics_07_variational_circuit_simulation`

## Research objective

For the supplied graphs, circuit depths, gate operations, and compression steps, compare errors and full-state fidelity between the classical representation and independent state-vector calculations. Determine the steps, graph types, and depth ranges in which results agree, are approximate, or fail, and state the extrapolation limits for scales not covered by the data.
