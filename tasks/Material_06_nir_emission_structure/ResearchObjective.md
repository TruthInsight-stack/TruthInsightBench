# State- and Composition-Dependent Long-Wavelength Emission Structure

- Task ID: `Material_06_nir_emission_structure`
- T0: `2020-01-01`

## Research objective

Study how emission spectra and long-wavelength response change with material state, design series, and composition. Compare peak shifts, broadening, monotone and nonmonotone composition effects, state-specific behavior, and unresolved explanations using complete-series and wavelength-band validation.
