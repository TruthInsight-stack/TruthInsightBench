## Research objective

Analyze unit-, site-, day-, symptom-, and assay-level relationships for reproducible environmental positivity patterns and failure boundaries. Compare site-specific, time-dependent, symptom-linked, quantitative, and no-stable-relation explanations using whole-unit holdouts.
