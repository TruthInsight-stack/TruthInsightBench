- Task ID: `Chemistry_07_photoionization_delay`

## Research objective

Study how the supplied phase, delay, magnitude, matrix-element, and angular-coefficient observations vary jointly with photon energy and emission angle. Identify reproducible regions, transitions, agreements, or disagreements between representations and between experiment-like and theory-like panels, while allowing for smooth, localized, competing, or unresolved explanations.
