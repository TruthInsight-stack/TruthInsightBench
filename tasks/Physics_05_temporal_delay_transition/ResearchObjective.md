# Buffer Parameters and Delay Dynamics in Temporal Networks

- Task ID: `Physics_05_temporal_delay_transition`
- T0: `2023-09-25`

## Research objective

Using the complete buffer scan, delay curves, and correlation functions, study how delay dynamics change with the buffer parameter. Compare smooth, segmented, nonlinear, no-identifiable-boundary, and finite-size-dependent explanations, and report sensitivity to fit windows, condition leave-out, and method changes.
