## Research objective

Using the supplied low-energy three-dimensional configurations and optimization trajectory, study how particle-size ratio, interfacial preference, and interparticle interaction strength relate to assembled structure. You may begin with reproducible features such as layering, interlayer position, composition, and in-plane order, or propose other data-supported structural descriptors. Compare explanations involving continuous change, structural regions, multiple solutions, or no stable relationship.
