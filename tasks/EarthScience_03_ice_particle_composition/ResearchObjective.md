# Depth-Resolved Particle Abundance and Composition in Ice Samples

- Task ID: `EarthScience_03_ice_particle_composition`
- T0: `2018-01-01`

## Research objective

Investigate how particle abundance, uncertainty, size composition, polymer composition, depth, and environmental covariates vary within and across collection groups. Seek relationships that survive whole-group and uncertainty-aware checks, and distinguish broad structure from group-specific anomalies or unresolved patterns.
