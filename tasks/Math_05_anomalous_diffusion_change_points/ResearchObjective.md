# Change-Point and Regime Structure in Anonymous Trajectories

- Task ID: `Math_05_anomalous_diffusion_change_points`
- T0: `2021-01-01`

## Research objective

Discover recurrent dynamical regimes, change points, scaling structure, and failure boundaries in the anonymous trajectories without assuming a fixed number of regimes. Compare stationary, piecewise, smoothly varying, and unresolved explanations using complete-trajectory and contiguous-time validation.
