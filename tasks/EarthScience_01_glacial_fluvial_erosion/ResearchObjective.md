# Scale and Measurement Boundaries of Glacial and Nonglacial Erosion Rates

- Task ID: `EarthScience_01_glacial_fluvial_erosion`
- T0: `2024-10-17`

## Research objective

Using the global compilation of glacial and nonglacial erosion rates, estimate how erosion rate relates to observation timescale, process category, and measurement method. Compare no-stable-relation, smooth scale dependence, method-stratified, and process-category explanations, while separating geological process, observation window, and mathematical coupling.
