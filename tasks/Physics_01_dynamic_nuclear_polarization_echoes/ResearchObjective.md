# Electron and Nuclear Spin Dynamics under Phase Inversion

- Task ID: `Physics_01_dynamic_nuclear_polarization_echoes`
- T0: `2024-06-26`

## Research objective

Apply phase cycling, echo integration, and signed-signal reconstruction to the supplied raw EPR and NMR scans, and characterize how the dynamics change with phase-inversion time. Estimate the temporal structure of the electron- and nuclear-spin readouts independently, then compare whether their relationship is stable, offset, condition-dependent, or indeterminate.
