# Self-Organization and Prediction Boundaries in Multi-Session Neural Networks

- Task ID: `Neuro_01_self_organization_prediction`
- T0: `2022-01-01`

## Research objective

Use multi-session latent-variable, stimulus, and neural-response time series from multiple independent trials to characterize their joint evolution. Compare early-observation models with simple persistence or mean baselines and with the possibility that no useful predictive relationship exists. Determine whether later responses or effective connectivity are predictable, and when that conclusion holds or fails across independent trials, conditions, and analysis definitions.
