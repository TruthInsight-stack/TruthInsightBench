# Structural Domains and Model Boundaries of Organic Bond Dissociation Enthalpies

- Task ID: `Chemistry_04_bond_enthalpy_domain`
- T0: `2019-10-18`

## Research objective

Using the complete computational database of homolytic bond dissociation enthalpies for small molecules and three estimation channels evaluated on the same experimental observations, characterize database coverage, major bond classes, and the error and failure boundaries of each channel. Report regions in which channels are better, equivalent, worse, or not distinguishable with the available sample.
