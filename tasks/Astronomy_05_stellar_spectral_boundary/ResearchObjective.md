## Research objective

Investigate how spectral response varies with wavelength, effective temperature, and the ordered composition index. Compare smooth, monotone, nonlinear, band-limited, temperature-specific, and unresolved explanations, and test them on complete spectral bands or temperature slices and the independent normalized series.
