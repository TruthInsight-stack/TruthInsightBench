# Cross-Modal Performance and Kinetic Tradeoffs in Photocatalytic Formulations

- Task ID: `Energy_01_photocatalytic_tradeoffs`
- T0: `2022-01-01`

## Research objective

Analyze how product evolution, optical/action response, ultrafast transients, and cofactor loading relate across the supplied anonymous formulations. Compare monotone, peaked, formulation-specific, cross-modal, and no-stable-relation explanations, and test their sensitivity to complete curve or spectral-window holdouts.
