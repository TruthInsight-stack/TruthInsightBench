# Sensor Conditions and Fusion Phenotypes in a Single-Vesicle System

- Task ID: `Life_01_dual_sensor_fusion`
- T0: `2024-01-01`

## Research objective

Given independent runs across sensor dosages, variants, competitor concentrations, and control conditions, characterize changes in total fusion at 5 seconds, early fusion timing, and molecular binding separately. Compare null, monotonic, non-monotonic, threshold, and condition-heterogeneous explanations, and report which relationships reproduce across runs and operational definitions.
